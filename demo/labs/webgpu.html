PlayJev in the browser

The game runs in the iframe below and every frame is answered by a vision language model running inside this tab on WebGPU. No server, no upload: the weights are downloaded once and the forward pass happens on your own GPU. The point of this page is the number in decisions per second.

The weights here are the stock onnx-community/Qwen3.5-0.8B-ONNX base model, not the PlayJev checkpoint, so the moves it picks are close to a letter prior. This page measures speed and proves the path; play quality needs our fine tuned weights exported to ONNX.
